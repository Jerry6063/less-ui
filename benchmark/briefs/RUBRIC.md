# Scoring rubric for skill A/B runs (0-2 each, 14 max)

Adapted from Nutlope/hallmark `site/_tests/README.md` plus the grading emphasis of timed AI-collaboration assessments
(business logic, feasibility, deliverable clarity).

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Structural distinctness | same hero-3-cards-CTA template | some variation | layout follows the content's own logic |
| 2 | Hierarchy and scanability | flat, everything shouts | mostly clear | one focal point per screen, 60-second read works |
| 3 | AI tells | purple gradients, Inter everywhere, identical cards, emoji icons | one or two tells | none of the listed tells |
| 4 | States | happy path only | some empty/loading/error | every data region has all three |
| 5 | Interaction fidelity | static | partial navigation | full clickable flow, drawer/tabs work |
| 6 | Business realism | lorem ipsum, fake KPIs | plausible | numbers, SKUs and copy a PM could present |
| 7 | Time and token cost | >25 min or heavy back-and-forth | 15-25 min | one pass under 15 min |

Record per run in `runs/<skill>/<brief>/notes.md`: wall-clock time, number of turns, score per row,
and one screenshot at 1440 and one at 375 where applicable.
